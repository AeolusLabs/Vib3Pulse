Vib3 Notes

You pay at the moment you spray, in Vib3Cash (V3C). No balance to top up or lose. Paper that flutters, a bag that splits open, one big moment when someone makes it rain, and a room that never loses its chat.

The notes

Three notes, then three bigger sprays. Rosettes are drawn from sound waves, never from a real banknote. Every note says it is not legal tender. Notes shimmer a little so they feel like objects.

Try the spray

Tap a person on stage to choose who you are spraying, then press Spray. You pay when you throw, with a card saved once. Everything here is a demo and nothing is charged.

Rules the effects follow

  • Chat always wins.The effects layer ignores touches, never covers the composer, and rain thins out when a live video is on screen.
  • Scale with the spend.A note is a whisper, a bundle is a gesture, Make it Rain is an event. Sizes of effect match sizes of spend.
  • Cheap to run.One canvas, pre-drawn sprites, at most 200 live notes, 2x pixel ratio cap. If frames slip, spawn rate drops. Pauses when the tab is hidden.
  • Easy to turn off.Quiet effects, reduced motion, and sound off all swap the animation for a chat line and a short chip.
  • Nothing is stored.You pay at the moment you spray. There is no balance to top up, expire, refund or lose. A spray that fails is never charged, and V3C is only a unit shown on the spray, not money held for you.
  • Safe by default.Adults only. No spraying under 18s, none in teens rooms, none from anonymous guests. Big sprays need a slide, a bank approval and a room limit.
