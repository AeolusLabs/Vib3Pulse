VIB3 ROOMS DESIGN PACK
======================

To view (no account or special software needed):
  1. Double-click  index.html  (opens in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
  2. Click the buttons to open each interactive prototype and the legal and accounting brief.
  3. On a phone: unzip first, then open index.html from the Files app, or just send the PDF.

What is in here:
  index.html ........ the overview with all key screens
  rooms.html ........ interactive Rooms prototype (chat, live video, immersive, join with age rules)
  notes.html ........ interactive spray prototype (pay at the moment you spray, bundle, money bag, Make it Rain)
  flows.html ........ every flow end to end, including a create-a-room wizard you can try
  brief.html ........ legal and accounting brief for Nigeria (version 2, pay-per-spray, no balance)
  Vib3-Rooms-Legal-Accounting-Brief-Nigeria.pdf ... the same brief as a PDF (best for phones and email)
  img/ .............. screenshots used by the overview

Sharing:
  - Easiest: send the PDF (the brief) and/or the whole zip file by email or WhatsApp.
  - To give someone a link: drag this folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop (free). Anyone with the link can view it.
    It is not password protected and the brief is confidential, so only do this if you are comfortable with that.

All people, messages, prices and numbers in the screens are sample data. Nothing is charged.
