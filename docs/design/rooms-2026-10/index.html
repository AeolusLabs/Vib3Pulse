Vib3 Rooms

A live room you can talk in, watch in and spray in. This pack shows the screens, the rules behind them and the open decisions. Every person and message is sample data. Nothing is charged. You pay at the moment you spray, in Vib3Cash (V3C): ₦1,000 = 100,000 V3C, shown in your local currency. There is no balance.

Chat comes first

People used to scroll past avatars to find the chat. Now chat takes every pixel the room does not need. Swipe a message right to reply, tap a name for the person sheet, and View profile is always the first option.

Joining, anonymously if you like

Age is checked on the account, on the server, every time. Anonymous is a mask: others see "Guest 4F2", but hosts and moderators still see who it is, and reports and bans follow the account.

Finding and starting a room

Rooms you are not old enough for are hidden, never shown locked. Starting a room is one sheet, then five short steps. What you can switch on depends on who you are and who the room is for.

Paid rooms, from price to payout

Hosts set the price and see their take before they publish. Buyers see every line, confirm they are an adult, and are refunded automatically if the room never starts. Ticket money is held until the room has happened, then paid.

Spraying, paid at the moment you throw

Three notes, a bundle, a money bag and Make it Rain. The notes are our own artwork, not real currency, and say so on their face. Small sprays are quick and quiet. Bags and Make it Rain take over more of the screen. Chat stays readable.

Hosting, safety and getting paid

Everything a host needs without leaving the room, the guest side of safety, and earnings that settle through Paystack to the host's own bank.

Eleven flows and about sixty screens in all, including notifications, invites, privacy settings, receipts, spending limits, reversed payments, and every error state. Open the end to end board to see them.

How the money is shared

Proposed, not final. Each payment is split at the moment of payment through Paystack, so a host's share goes towards their bank and we never hold it. Splits apply to the amount left after VAT (7.5%), card fees and a fraud reserve: about 88 to 89 kobo of every naira on a local card.

Who handles the money

Paystack, for Nigeria. Sprays are split at source. Ticket money is held briefly, then paid to the host. Stripe for the UK later. We never pool funds in our own bank account for sprays.

Safety rules

  • Adults only. Nothing to or from under 18s, nothing in teens rooms, nothing from anonymous guests.
  • Room limits, a slide to pay, bank approval for large sprays, and limits on new hosts.

What V3C is

A unit shown on the price of a spray. It is not stored for anyone, has no cash value and cannot be moved or cashed out. You pay at the moment you spray.

Still to decide

  • Note values. The pack assumes the notes are ₦20, ₦50 and ₦100 (2,000, 5,000 and 10,000 V3C). Paystack recommends at least ₦50 per charge, so either ₦50 becomes the smallest note, or small notes are batched into one charge.
  • The name. "Vib3Cash" and "spray" sit close to the naira-spraying offence. The brief recommends a name that does not say cash.
  • Money only in 18+ rooms. Paid rooms and spray are limited to 18+ and 21+ rooms with an adult host.
  • Anonymous teens on the mic in teens-only rooms. The proposal says no, in every room.
  • Legal and tax review in Nigeria before any money feature is built: whether the per-spray, split-at-source structure needs any CBN approval, agent treatment, VAT, and age verification.
  • Launch in phases. Adults and free rooms first, then paid rooms, then sprays, then teens. See the brief, section 9.
