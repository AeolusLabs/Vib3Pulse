What this decides

Spray goes to the player
Notes land on the tile you tap and add to that player's total. The split bar shows who the room is backing. It tips a person. It is never a bet on the result, and the tray says so.
Three ways on screen
Share a window from Chrome or Edge on PC. OBS with a stream key, which also covers consoles through a capture card. Phone waits for the native app and shows as coming soon.
Cheap to watch
Each tile pulls only the quality its size needs: 720p when big, 360p in a versus pair, 180p in a thumbnail. Turn on Show stream layers to see it, and the line beside it adds up what one viewer downloads.
Team games, 2v2 to 4v4
Every player shares their own screen. The room shows one featured feed per team and the rest as small thumbnails. The host picks the featured feed; any viewer can tap a thumbnail to follow a player on their own device, which changes only what that viewer downloads. Feeds nobody is watching stop uploading.
Spray a player or a team
Spray a player from their feed, or spray the whole team and the amount splits equally between players still in the match. The tray shows the share per player before you throw.
Age lanes
All ages can join, but each age group gets its own lane: 18+ and 13–17. Matches, chat, spray and viewer counts stay inside the lane, so adults and teens never see or message each other. Use View as to switch.
When a player drops
Their feed holds the slot and shows Reconnecting with a countdown. If the host set a timeout and it runs out, the room posts "[name] has dropped out". With no timeout, the slot stays as it is until they return.
Host decides who plays
Players ask to go live, the host accepts, up to four on screen. Remove, spotlight and mute game audio sit in the host console. Teen and adult rules stay the same as the stage.
Safe to share
The go-live sheet tells players to share the game window, not the whole screen, because DMs and notifications can show up on stream.

Mockup only. Spray amounts, the limit and the stream key are example values. Real payouts stay behind the existing payments gate.
